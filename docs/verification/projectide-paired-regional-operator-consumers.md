Original capture limits, actual traversal counters and later view limits remain separate. Missing Requirement evidence does not gain a condition graph. ## Lifecycle and scope

Both consumers withdraw selection, metadata and bodies across Source, input, result and recording changes. Generation and exact-member checks prevent late reads from replacing newer selections. Native notification reentry, cancellation and reset and Angular permission loss, protected clearing and destruction are covered by the paired tests. Caller-retained immutable original snapshots do not become current views.

These are local synthetic inspection contracts. They add no remote record deserializer, endpoint, Registry, current authorization, producer-trust, eligibility or retention-policy adoption. Actual Avalonia UI, Native/Production/Release adoption and existing wider HOLDs remain separate. Security/shared-CI work remains with its existing P0 owners.
